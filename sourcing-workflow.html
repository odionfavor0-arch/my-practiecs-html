Hybrid sourcing playbook

Buy one sample from 1688 through Womata, fly it in with Sky Cargo, shoot your own content, test with CJ fulfilment, and only move a product to bulk 1688 stock once the numbers prove it.

The workflow

Demand first, then product, then supplier. Each phase ends with a gate: don't spend money on the next phase until the gate is passed.

Find demand

Phase 0 · Ongoing
  1. Collect candidates from Amazon UK/US best-seller and "movers" lists, TikTok Shop and TikTok Creative Center. Don't start by browsing CJ. That's supplier-first thinking.
  2. Check each one in Meta Ad Library (are several advertisers running it for 30+ days?) and Google Trends over 90 days (rising, stable or falling?).
  3. Check the cheapest price on Jumia and Konga, and ask whether it's sold in Balogun or Computer Village.
  4. Log everything in the product research sheet and rank by evidence.

Gate: strong demand signals, and your Nigerian price is no more than about 1.5× the typical Jumia price.

Find and price

Phase 1 · Days 1–3
  1. Take a top-ranked candidate from the research sheet and search for it on 1688. Starting on 1688 means scrolling through millions of listings with no demand signal.
  2. Shortlist 3 suppliers for the same item and paste each link into Womata. Write down the CNY price, MOQ, product weight and Womata's quote for each.
  3. Find the matching CJ listing (see checklist below) and note CJ's product + shipping cost to Nigeria.
  4. Run both numbers through the max price calculator.

Gate: the CJ route leaves a profit at your planned selling price after ad cost. If it only works on bulk 1688 pricing, you can't test it cheaply, so skip it.

Sample

Phase 2 · Weeks 1–3
  1. Order ONE sample through Womata. If two suppliers look close, order from both and compare.
  2. Ship via Sky Cargo. Group several samples into one shipment where you can, since per-kg freight often has a minimum charge.
  3. While it's in transit, set up the Shopify product page using the CJ listing and your own copy.
  4. When it lands: check build quality against the 1688 photos, weigh it, and note every flaw a customer would complain about.

Gate: you'd be happy receiving this at your selling price. If not, drop it now. This is the whole reason for the sample.

Content and test

Phase 3 · Weeks 3–6
  1. Shoot 10–20 short videos with the sample: unboxing, problem/solution, before/after, the product in a Nigerian home or street setting.
  2. Link the Shopify product to the CJ variant so orders auto-route to CJ.
  3. Post organically for a week, then put a fixed test budget behind the 2–3 best performers.
  4. Track cost per purchase, not views or likes.

Gate: cost per purchase is at or below the figure you put in the calculator, across at least 10 orders.

Scale to local stock

Phase 4 · When orders are steady
  1. Once you're doing roughly 30+ orders a month on one product, run the CJ vs bulk comparison below.
  2. Place a first bulk order sized to about 4–6 weeks of sales, not a year's worth.
  3. Air freight via Sky Cargo for the first batch (speed), consider sea freight once demand is predictable.
  4. Hold stock at home or with a Lagos 3PL and switch the Shopify product to local fulfilment. Keep CJ as backup if you sell out.

Gate: bulk landed cost beats CJ by enough to cover the cash tied up in stock and the risk of leftover units.

The weak spot in this plan is payment. A large share of Nigerian online shoppers want pay-on-delivery, and CJ can't collect cash for you. During the CJ phase you'll be selling prepaid only (Paystack or Flutterwave on Shopify), with 7–20 day delivery. That will lower your conversion rate compared with sellers offering local stock and pay-on-delivery, so treat CJ-phase results as a floor. A product that sells prepaid with slow shipping will usually do better once it's local.

Finding the product on 1688

1688 is in Chinese and built for Chinese wholesale buyers, so search differently from AliExpress.

Search by image

Take a screenshot of the product from TikTok or AliExpress and use 1688's camera/image search icon in the search bar. This is faster and more accurate than keywords.

Search by Chinese keyword

Put the English name into Google Translate, copy the Chinese, and search with it. Try 2–3 variations (for example "posture corrector" might be 矫姿带 or 驼背矫正带).

Pick a supplier

Prefer listings showing 实力商家 (verified strong merchant) or 源头工厂 (source factory), a long store age, high repeat-purchase rate (回头率) and real buyer photos. Very cheap listings for the same item are often thinner material or a smaller size, which the sample will reveal.

Record it

For each shortlisted listing, save the link, the CNY price at your likely order quantity, MOQ, weight (重量) and dimensions. You'll need weight for freight.

Is the CJ listing the same product?

CJ and 1688 often sell from the same factories, but titles and photos get changed. Tick what matches. Five or more and you can treat it as the same item; below that, request a CJ sample or pick a different listing.
