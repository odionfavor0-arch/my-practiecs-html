## Video direction

- **Palette system** — from `frame.md` (Bold Poster, remixed): `dark` (#1A1A1A) ink on `light` (#FFFFFF) canvas; `accent` is the brand pink (#FD6890, sampled from the PHASR logo) — the ONLY accent color anywhere in the film, on every key word, mark, glow, and UI chrome. Never substitute another hue for it.
- **Motion grammar + reveal model** — long-tail `power3` eases throughout (smooth, not bouncy). Every frame reveals piece-by-piece cued to its spoken line — nothing appears before the VO says it; reveals spread across the back ~50% of each frame's window. Holds stay still (subtle jitter at most) — no lazy breathing, no forced drift.
- **Rhythm / held-frame allocation** — Frame 3 ("you become who you say you are") is the held climax of the first act: the thesis line lands, then holds still and full-bleed before the LARP/proof beats. Frame 7 (logo) holds still on the final card — no motion competes with the tagline read.
- **Negative list** — no stock-photo aesthetics, no generic floating "AI" gradient blobs, no purple/blue substituting for brand pink, no front-loaded-then-frozen frames, no continuous screensaver drift on a static reveal, no nav bars / browser chrome / real cursors.
- **SFX allocation** (no BGM bed, so these carry the energy): soft riser out of Frame 1 into Frame 2 (topic turn); subtle whoosh into Frame 5 (proof-flash montage begins); warm chime on Frame 6 entrance (PHASR app UI reveal).
