## Intent

A ~38s vertical promo for PHASR, a goal-setting/identity app. Opens with a
confident, contrarian neuroscience/psychology hook (self-perception theory,
"LARPing" your identity) before revealing that PHASR turns that identity
work into daily tasks. Confident and a little contrarian at the open, warm
and motivating by the close. VO is verbatim — narrate the client's script
exactly as written, do not restructure or paraphrase it.

- ../../pink logo phasr.jpeg — PHASR logo, glossy 3D pink arrow/swoosh mark. Sample its exact pink as the brand's signature accent color used throughout the whole video, not just the end card.

- Three quick glowing-silhouette flashes (athlete visualization pose, founder at a whiteboard, soldier in formation) around 0:24-0:29 — stylized outlines, not literal/identifiable people, illustrating "athletes, founders, military — same technique."
