How the score works

Demand is Amazon + TikTok + Meta (0–3 each) plus Google Trends (rising 3, stable 2, falling 0). That's up to 12.

Margin adds up to 5: after landed cost and ad cost, 30%+ of the selling price left scores 5, 20% scores 3, 10% scores 1. Competition adds 3 for low, 2 for medium, 0 for high.

One hard rule overrides the score: if your price is more than 1.5× the typical Jumia price, the verdict is "Jumia undercuts" no matter how strong demand looks. Nigerian shoppers compare.
